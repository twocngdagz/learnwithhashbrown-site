Video Templates

Video Templates is the private tool the owner of Learn with Hash Brown uses to turn the family's own phone videos into short edited videos. It reads the videos he puts in his own Google Drive, describes them so the right clips can be found, and saves the finished edits back to the same Google Drive.

It has one user, its owner. Nothing is shared, published or sold.
