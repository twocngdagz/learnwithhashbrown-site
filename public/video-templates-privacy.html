Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers Video Templates, the private tool the owner of Learn with Hash Brown uses to make edited videos from his family's own videos.

What it accesses and why

When the owner signs in with Google, Video Templates asks to read files in his Google Drive, so it can see the videos he puts in his own folders, and to create files, so it can save descriptions of those videos and the finished edits in folders it made itself. It does not change or delete any other file.

How the data is used

The videos are used only to make the owner's own edited videos. Small preview copies are kept on his computer, and AI services he chose describe those previews so the right clips can be found. Video Templates's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

What is stored

The descriptions and finished edits are stored in the owner's Google Drive and on his computer. The sign-in token Google issues is stored only in his computer's password store.

Children

The family's videos include children, but Video Templates is used only by the adult owner, and the videos stay in his own Google Drive and on his own computer.

Removing access

The owner can remove Video Templates's access at any time from his Google Account's security settings, and can delete the copies on his computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
