Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers Hash Brown Uploader, the private tool the owner of the Learn with Hash Brown YouTube channel uses to upload lesson videos to that channel.

YouTube API Services

Hash Brown Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses and why

When the channel owner signs in, Hash Brown Uploader asks Google for one permission: to upload videos to his YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to upload the channel's own lesson videos and set their title, description and visibility. It does not read, collect or store any other information from Google or YouTube.

What is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so the tool can upload without signing in every time. Nothing is sent to any other server, shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising. No information about viewers of the channel is collected.

Children

The lessons are for children, but Hash Brown Uploader is used only by the adult channel owner. It collects no information from or about children.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove Hash Brown Uploader's access at any time from his Google Account's security settings, and can delete the stored token from his computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
