Learn with Hash Brown

Learn with Hash Brown makes short animated maths and science lessons for primary school children, presented by Hash Brown the owl. The lessons are published on the Learn with Hash Brown YouTube channel.

Hash Brown Uploader

Hash Brown Uploader is a small private tool that the channel's owner runs on his own computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the channel's finished lesson videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the Learn with Hash Brown channel. It is used only by the channel's owner, only for his own channel, and is not offered to anyone else.
